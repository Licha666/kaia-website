Our story

A little piece of Georgia,
in the heart of Barcelona

In Georgian, ქაი (k’ai) means good — and that is exactly what we set out to serve. Good food, good wine, and the famously generous Georgian welcome. Every khachapuri is baked to order, every khinkali pleated by hand, and every glass of wine poured from bottles made with grapes grown in 8,000-year-old qvevri traditions.

Set on Carrer de Mallorca in the Eixample, KAIA is a place to linger — over long supras with friends, soulful background music, and plates made for sharing.

Menu highlights

The dishes our guests come back for — baked, pleated and grilled the Georgian way.

Adjaruli Khachapuri

€12

Boat-shaped bread filled with molten sulguni cheese, finished with a golden egg yolk and butter. Stir, tear, dip — enjoy.

Khinkali

€2.8 / pc

Hand-pleated soup dumplings with spiced beef and pork. Hold by the knot, sip the broth, then take the bite. Sold in fours.

From the Grill

€14

Mtsvadi skewers and grilled vegetables with halloumi — a guest favourite, served straight off the coals.

Pkhali & Starters

Walnut-and-spinach pkhali, creamy badrijani eggplant rolls and warm fresh bread — made for the middle of the table.

Kharcho

Hearty beef and walnut soup, slow-cooked with tkemali plum and warm spices. Ask for bread to mop up every last drop.
