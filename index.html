Our story

A little piece of Georgia,
in the heart of Barcelona

In Georgian, კაი (k’ai) means good — and that is exactly what we set out to serve. Good food, good wine, and the famously generous Georgian welcome. Every khachapuri is baked to order, every khinkali pleated by hand, and every glass of wine poured from bottles made with grapes grown in 8,000-year-old qvevri traditions.

Set on Carrer de Mallorca in the Eixample, KAIA is a place to linger — over long supras with friends, soulful background music, and plates made for sharing.
